Effective as of 2026-04-05

## Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include:

- Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
- The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the time spent on those pages
- The time spent on the Application
- The operating system you use on your mobile device
- Photos and images you upload of your clothing items and outfits
- Camera and photo library access to capture and select images of your clothing items

The Application does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device.

The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide features.

The Service Provider may use the information you provided to contact you from time to time to provide you with important information, required notices and marketing promotions.

For a better experience, while using the Application, the Service Provider may require you to provide certain personally identifiable information. The information that the Service Provider requests will be retained by them and used as described in this privacy policy.

## Camera and Photo Library Access

The Application requires access to your device's camera and photo library to allow you to upload images of your clothing items. You may upload photos by taking a new photo with your camera or by selecting an existing photo from your device's photo library.

All images are stored locally on your device only and are never uploaded to any external server or cloud service. The Service Provider does not upload, store, or have access to any photos or images on any external server or cloud service.

The Service Provider does not share, sell, or use your photos for any purpose other than providing the Application's core functionality.

You may revoke camera and photo library access at any time through your device's Settings app. Please note that revoking this access will limit the functionality of the Application.

The Application stores all data locally on your device only. This includes all clothing items, outfit combinations, and photos you upload. No personal data, images, or user content is transmitted to or stored on any external server or cloud service.
